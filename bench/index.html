Per-task tables — the losses are the point

Both lanes see byte-identical questions. The modelless lane is ns–µs tier and bit-deterministic on a given build; the open-weights laya lane is the accuracy heavyweight (rust = our port, python = the original torch reference). No single "we win" number exists or will exist — audit the tables. The modelless lane's near-chance rows on out-of-domain text are structural (it is corpus-bound by design), and they are published here rather than trimmed. Read the modelless lane on its own axes — latency, and selective accuracy under abstention (acc@50cov, abstain/sel-acc) — not raw accuracy against laya. The G1 line gates calibration honesty only (does the fitted confidence beat the raw readout AND the conformal-naive floor?) — it is not an accuracy gate; a FAIL there means the calibrator had nothing to improve, never that the answers got worse.
